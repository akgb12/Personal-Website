## Display guidance

Preferred:
- large text stream / moving editorial bands
- graduate and undergraduate visually distinguished
- course code secondary to title or vice versa depending on composition
- continuous motion may respond subtly to scroll velocity
- course text must remain readable and accessible

Avoid:
- dozens of pills/chips
- tiny 4-column course cards
- transcript-table styling
